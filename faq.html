新手常见问题 FAQ

玩 Islets 之前最想问的十几个问题，先看这里。剧透等级：无到轻；遇到含剧情名字的 Q&A 会明确标注。

新手 FAQ 最后校验：2026-09-01 阅读约 4 分钟
使用方式：点击问题展开答案。如果你想从更体系化的角度了解玩法，先看 新手开局指南。

基础机制

Q1. 磁力核心是什么？怎么用？

每座独立岛屿深处都有一个"磁力核心"。激活后，两座岛屿会以一段剧情动画连接回主地图，并立即在交界处生成新的通道和隐藏房间。它是 Islets 的核心解谜机制，等同于"主线推进 + 解锁新探索面"。

Q2. 死亡会扣钱、扣升级、扣魂吗？

不会。Islets 采用零惩罚死亡：Iko 死亡后从最近存档点复活，资源、货币、永久升级都不掉。请放心尝试跳跃、敢于跑酷。

Q3. 通关一次要多久？

HowLongToBeat 数据：主线 约 7.5 小时，主线 + 支线 约 8 小时，全收集 约 9.5 小时。每天玩 1–2 小时，一周左右可通关。

Q4. 难度可以中途改吗？

可以。开始新游戏时选择辅助/挑战滑杆，进入游戏后也随时能在暂停菜单 → Options → Difficulty 里调整受击伤害、攻击输出、敌人血量等。

Q5. 有中文吗？

有。游戏支持 简体中文 文本（含 NPC 对话、信件、菜单），无语音；其他支持语言含英、法、德、西、葡、俄。

玩法与卡关

Q6. 看到够不着的平台/打不破的墙，要硬打吗？

不要硬打。Islets 大量使用"能力门"设计：够不着的高台通常是二段跳/攀墙/云之箭的事；打不破的金属箱要等下劈（蓄力攻击）。遇到障碍，先在脑中打勾、继续前进，拿到新能力再回头看。

Q7. 同一个 Boss 反复打不过怎么办？

三步检查：

  1. 当前区域是否还有未走过的出口？换个方向探索可能直接绕过。
  2. 最近拿到的能力通常既能影响移动也影响战斗，想想它的另一种用法。
  3. 先去找几个 永久升级（剑、箭、生命），再回来挑战——加 1 颗心可能就够了。

死亡零惩罚 + 难度可调，放心尝试。

Q8. 飞艇战打不过，要怎么练？

飞艇战是 bullet hell，重点是认准小命中圈（红圈/中央小圆），整艘船不是你的判定。观察敌人弹道规律，先求"不被命中"，等反弹弹回或武器就位再输出。前期空中海盗那场武器都没装，等它扔出"回旋镖"打到自己就赢。

Q9. 必须按顺序打 BOSS 吗？

主线岛屿的顺序基本固定，但连接岛屿后回头探索很自由。BOSS Rush 则需要先通关一次解锁。

Q10. 永久升级三选一怎么选？

没有"标准答案"。刚在某类挑战受挫就补对应短板——常被 Boss 摸死优先 生命，常被远处敌人打优先 箭矢，常打不动怪优先 剑。本页用单独的 升级思路 给推荐。

Q11. 货币怎么花最划算？

Sky City 的商店里几乎所有东西都"有用"，没有"骗钱选项"。通常推荐优先：地图标记（找不到目标时省时间）> 飞艇升级（影响空战容错）> 剑/箭/生命升级。

Q12. 通关之后还能玩什么？

通关解锁 Boss Rush（普通 / 困难 / 无伤）以及"NPC 信件 / 支线任务 / 隐藏房间"的剩余内容。全 Steam 成就 44 个，覆盖所有内容。

轻度剧透：能力相关

Q13. 第一个拿到的能力是二段跳吗？

接近。二段跳（Double Jump）是 Iko 在早期主线里通过剧情和挑战获得的第一项关键移动能力，从此竖向探索空间打开。建议回头把之前的岔路再走一遍。

Q14. 攀墙能力什么时候拿到？

在主线推进过程中拿到二段跳后不久。具体先后顺序请参考 永久升级与能力（P1 专题攻略准备中）。

Q15. "云之箭"是什么？

特定箭矢升级后，射出的箭会留下一道云状平台，可站立走过缺口或挡住部分陷阱。是后期"考验眼力"型解谜和战斗的常用解法。

Core mechanics

Q1. What is a magnetic core?

Every detached isle hides a magnetic core. Activating it triggers a short cinematic that fuses the isle back into the world map and immediately opens new passages at the seam. It's Islets' main progression + exploration mechanic.

Q2. Do I lose anything on death?

No. Islets uses a no-penalty death model: Iko respawns at the last save with all currency, upgrades, and progress intact. Feel free to experiment with jumps and combat.

Q3. How long is the game?

HowLongToBeat medians: main story ~7.5 h, main + extras ~8 h, completionist ~9.5 h. At 1–2 hours per day, you can finish the main story in about a week.

Q4. Can I change difficulty mid-run?

Yes. Pick assist / challenge sliders at new game, and you can also adjust inbound damage, outbound damage, and enemy HP at any time in Options → Difficulty.

Q5. Is there Chinese language support?

Yes — full Simplified Chinese text (NPC, letters, menus). No voice acting. Other languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese (BR), Russian.

Gameplay and getting stuck

Q6. Should I brute-force unreachable ledges and breakable walls?

No. Islets uses a lot of "ability gating". Ledges usually need Double Jump / Wall Climb / Cloud Shot; metal crates need the Plunge. When blocked, mentally check it off and keep moving — come back with the right ability.

Q7. What do I do when a boss keeps beating me?

Three checks:

  1. Is there an unexplored exit in this area? Bypassing can be faster than retrying.
  2. Your latest ability usually affects both movement and combat; rethink it.
  3. Grab a few permanent upgrades (sword, bow, health) and try again — one more heart is often the difference.

Death is free and difficulty is adjustable.

Q8. How do I practice airship fights?

Airship combat is bullet-hell; your hitbox is the small inner circle, not the whole ship. Learn enemy patterns, prioritise survival, and wait for the boss's boomerang / cannon windows. The first Sky Pirates fight has no weapon on your side — survive until its boomerang returns to damage it.

Q9. Do I have to fight bosses in a fixed order?

The main island path is largely linear, but backtracking is free after each fusion. Boss Rush requires finishing the main game first.

Q10. How should I pick from the three upgrade options?

There's no single "correct" pick. Address the challenge that just frustrated you: Health if bosses chunk you, Arrows if ranged enemies pick you off, Sword if combat feels slow. See the upcoming upgrade guide for more detail.

Q11. Best way to spend currency?

Almost every shop item is useful. Common priority: map markers (saves time when lost) > airship upgrades (improve air combat) > sword / arrow / health upgrades.

Q12. What can I do after finishing the game?

Beat the main story to unlock Boss Rush (Normal / Hard / No-hit) and revisit the remaining letters, side quests, and hidden rooms. The full Steam set is 44 achievements.

Light spoilers: abilities

Q13. Is Double Jump the first ability?

Approximately. Double Jump is the first major movement ability earned through the early story, opening up most vertical exploration. It's worth backtracking after acquiring it.

Q14. When do I get Wall Climb?

Shortly after Double Jump through the main story. Exact order will be detailed in the upcoming abilities & upgrades page.

Q15. What is Cloud Shot?

An arrow upgrade that leaves a cloud platform in its wake, useful for crossing gaps and blocking certain traps. It becomes a go-to for tricky puzzles and combat spacing.
