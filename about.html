关于本站About This Guide

这里是 GAME NOTE 旗下的《Islets》中英文攻略站，整理主线流程、地图、首领、能力、收集品与探索资料，帮助玩家快速定位当前进度和需要解决的问题。This is GAME NOTE's bilingual Islets guide, covering the walkthrough, maps, bosses, abilities, collectibles and exploration references so players can quickly identify their current objective.

内容原则Content approach

中文名称尽量与游戏官方中文版保持一致；位置与流程说明会结合游戏画面和地图进行整理。攻略可能包含主线、首领及奖励剧透。Chinese terminology follows the official localization where possible. Locations and routes are organized using in-game scenes and maps. Guides may contain story, boss and reward spoilers.

资料更正Corrections

如果发现名称、位置、打法或奖励有误，欢迎提供游戏截图、所在岛屿和具体路线，我们会继续核对和完善。If a name, location, strategy or reward is inaccurate, please send an in-game screenshot, island name and route details so the entry can be checked and improved.
