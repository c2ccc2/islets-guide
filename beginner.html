新手开局与探索指南

从战斗、升级到磁力核心与能力回访，快速建立完整、顺畅的游玩循环。

新手攻略 最后校验：2026-09-01 阅读约 4 分钟
这款游戏的核心循环：在岛屿中探索 → 找到磁力核心并让岛屿连接 → 获得新路线与挑战 → 以新能力回访此前到不了的地方。

先掌握这 6 项基础操作

移动与跳跃

短按跳跃适合微调，长按能获得更高的上升距离。落向窄平台时提前松开方向。

近战攻击

普通敌人以短连击为主，命中两三次便观察反击，不要一直站在原地攻击。

翻滚闪避

用来穿过攻击并重置站位。最好朝敌人身后或空旷区域滚，不要把自己送进墙角。

瞄准与弓箭

先拉开距离再瞄准，优先处理飞行敌人、远程敌人和站在危险平台上的目标。

地图

房间边缘的出口线代表相邻通道；看见暂时打不开的出口，记住位置后继续主线。

互动与检查点

经过检查点及时激活。进入首领房或长平台挑战前，先确认附近是否有未触发的检查点。

开局推荐游玩顺序

01 跟随可达出口先熟悉移动、近战和弓箭，不必强行跳上明显过高的平台。
02 探索诺斯特布尔开启沿途检查点，清理顺路挑战，推进至墓地爬行者。
03 获得二段跳击败首领后用新能力检查刚才够不到的高台，再推进岛屿连接。
04 返回天空之城查看邮件、商店与下一条航线，再按主线章节继续。

刚开始时，优先做什么？

01 / 看见岔路，先记住

不是每条路都要当场走完

遇到够不着的平台、无法打破的障碍或明显缺少工具的机关，通常意味着它在等待后续能力。先在地图上留下印象，继续推进主路径即可。

02 / 战斗与远程交替

把弓箭当作节奏工具

Iko 同时拥有近战、闪避与远程箭矢。近身攻击适合稳定输出；面对危险落点、远处敌人或需要拉开距离的场面，优先给自己留出走位空间。

03 / 完成挑战再选升级

升级是长期收益，不必急着追求"最优解"

完成跑酷或战斗挑战会获得永久升级，并从多个选项中选择一个。生命、箭矢和攻击相关选项都能改善体验；若刚在某类挑战受挫，就优先补足对应短板。

04 / 重启核心后回看地图

"连接"本身就是解谜的一部分

岛屿连回主地图后，新的通道和回访路径会出现。每次完成一个磁力核心，先花一分钟检查相邻区域，再决定是继续主线还是回收此前留下的岔路。

卡关时的三步检查

  1. 确认当前区域是否还有未尝试过的出口，而不是反复挑战同一个跳跃点。
  2. 回忆最近拿到的能力：它通常既影响移动，也会在战斗或旧区域中创造新解法。
  3. 如果 Boss 让你持续受挫，先去探索可达岔路。额外升级会让下一次尝试更从容。

获得新能力后的回访原则

二段跳、攀墙、蓄力攻击与箭矢相关能力都会改变可探索范围。不要试图在获得前清空每一个问号；新的移动方式不仅开门，也会让战斗回避与反击有更多选择。

Learn these six basics first

Move & jump

Tap for a short hop and hold for more height. Release direction early when landing on a narrow platform.

Melee

Use short strings, then watch for retaliation. Do not remain planted while repeatedly attacking.

Dodge roll

Roll through attacks and reset your position. Aim for open ground or behind an enemy, not into a corner.

Aim & bow

Create space before aiming. Prioritise flying enemies, ranged enemies and targets on dangerous platforms.

Map

Ticks along a room edge indicate exits. Remember blocked exits and continue the story until you gain the needed ability.

Interact & checkpoints

Activate checkpoints as you pass them. Before a boss or long platform trial, check for one nearby.

Recommended opening route

01 Follow reachable exitsLearn movement, melee and bow basics; ignore ledges that are clearly too high.
02 Explore NorthStableActivate checkpoints, clear convenient trials and advance to Grave Crawler.
03 Gain Double JumpAfter the boss, revisit nearby high ledges before completing the island connection.

What should I prioritise first?

01 / NOTICE THE BRANCH

You do not need to clear every path immediately

An unreachable ledge, a breakable-looking barrier, or a mechanism missing a clear tool usually signals a later ability. Make a mental map note and continue along the route you can currently progress.

02 / MIX MELEE AND RANGE

Use arrows to control the pace

Iko has melee attacks, a dodge, and ranged arrows. Melee is dependable damage; for dangerous landings, distant enemies, or any time you need space, create that space first.

03 / FINISH CHALLENGES, THEN CHOOSE

Upgrades are lasting gains—not a test with one right answer

Platforming and combat challenges award permanent upgrades from several choices. Health, arrows, and attack-related options all improve comfort; choose the one that addresses the kind of challenge that just gave you trouble.

04 / CHECK THE MAP AFTER A CORE

Connection is part of the puzzle

Joining an isle back to the main map creates routes and return paths. After each magnetic core, spend a minute checking neighbouring areas before choosing between the main route and a saved branch.

A three-step check when you are stuck

  1. Look for an untried exit in the current area instead of retrying one jump indefinitely.
  2. Recall your latest ability: it commonly changes movement and creates a new answer in combat or an older area.
  3. If a boss remains overwhelming, explore reachable branches first. Extra upgrades make the next attempt calmer.

Where to return after gaining a new ability

Double Jump, Wall Climb, Charged Strike, and arrow upgrades all change your reachable range. Don't try to clear every question mark before getting them; new movement doesn't just open doors, it also gives you more ways to dodge and counter.
