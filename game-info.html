主角 Iko 与世界观

《Islets》的舞台是一片漂浮在天空中的群岛世界。很久以前这里曾是一整块完整的大陆，被勤劳的小动物们依靠巨型电磁装置安稳地连接在一起。后来某次意外让这些装置停摆，群岛四散漂移，每座小岛变得日益荒凉，怪物也借机滋生。

主角 Iko（伊科） 是一只怀揣梦想的鼠类小战士，立志要把散落的天空岛屿重新连回一起，让世界重归完整。他驾驶着略显破旧但忠诚的小飞艇，以 Sky City 为基地，逐个重启岛屿上的磁力核心。

旅途中你会遇到形形色色的角色：

  • Snoot（斯努特）：爱炫耀的同辈，嘴上不服、动作挑事。
  • Greybird（灰鸟）：热心又可靠的朋友，会在路上给你提示。
  • 完全正常的导游（Totally Normal Tour Guide）：官方吐槽的可疑角色，官方文案提示"vibes are weird"。
  • 还有更多藏在各岛屿的支线 NPC，他们负责氛围、彩蛋和小故事。
温馨提示：本作整体调性偏温馨，战斗虽存在但怪物行为并不血腥；适合作为下班 / 课后的放松型 Metroidvania。

类型、节奏与一句话评价

《Islets》属于"线性偏多、可探索为辅"的 Metroidvania：主线岛屿顺序基本固定，但每次连接新岛屿后，老地图会出现新的捷径和隐藏房间。整体节奏不紧绷，平台跳跃温和、Boss 设计具挑战但不刁难。Metacritic 评分 82/100，Steam 玩家评价 Overwhelmingly Positive。

Iko and the world

The world of Islets is an archipelago floating in the sky. Long ago the islands formed a single landmass, held together by giant electromagnetic devices tended by industrious critters. When those devices failed, the islands drifted apart, grew barren, and gave monsters a place to thrive.

Iko is a hopeful mouse warrior who dreams of reconnecting every isle. Piloting a rickety but loyal airship, he works out of Sky City and reignites each isle's magnetic core.

Along the way you'll meet a colourful cast:

  • Snoot — a boastful peer rival.
  • Greybird — a kind, recurring ally.
  • Totally Normal Tour Guide — the "weird vibes" character the store page teases.
  • Various side NPCs tucked into each biome, adding flavour and short vignettes.
Tone: The game is wholesome overall. Combat is present but not gory — a great evening Metroidvania.

Genre, pacing, and verdict

Islets is a fairly linear Metroidvania with strong backtracking rewards: the main path is set, but every successful fusion opens new shortcuts and hidden rooms. Platforming is forgiving; bosses are challenging but fair. Metacritic: 82/100. Steam: Overwhelmingly Positive.
