商店与 NPCShops & NPCs

查看天空之城的永久强化、地图服务与飞艇升级，以及会通过邮件更新位置的支线角色。

Find Sky City stat upgrades, map services, airship upgrades, and side characters whose locations update through mail.

STEP 01先买地图标记Map Markers first

花费 100，减少基础探索漏房间。Costs 100 and reduces missed rooms.

STEP 02中期买传送服务Teleport service midgame

花费 900，频繁回访时节省路线时间。Costs 900 and saves time during backtracking.

STEP 03全收集买升级标记Upgrade Markers for cleanup

花费 2500，只标记已经探索过的区域。Costs 2500 and only marks explored areas.

推荐购买顺序：先买基础地图标记；做 60 个永久升级全收集时再攒 2500 货币购买升级标记。生命、剑和箭矢强化按当前短板购买。Suggested order: Buy basic map markers first. Save 2500 currency for Upgrade Markers when hunting all 60 upgrades. Buy health, sword and arrow boosts according to your weakest stat.
