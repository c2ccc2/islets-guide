ISLETS · LETTER QUEST

神秘信件支线Mysterious Letter Quest

这是一条由永久升级三选一触发的连续寻宝线。每一步都会把你引向下一处地图地点。A chained treasure hunt triggered by a permanent-upgrade choice. Each letter points to the next map location.

触发条件：挑战获得永久升级时，选择“收到一封奇怪的信”（而不是普通数值强化）。随后回天空之城查看邮箱。Trigger: When a challenge offers “Receive a strange letter” beside normal stat boosts, choose it, then check the mailbox in Sky City.
01
天空之城 · 邮箱SKY CITY · MAILBOX

第一封：选择奇怪的信First letter: choose the strange letter

获得支线升级后回到商店区邮箱，阅读新信件。信件会提示第一处墓碑地点。Return to the shop district mailbox after taking the quest upgrade. Read the new letter for the first gravestone clue.

无能力要求No ability required
02
灵魂之地 · 东南侧墓碑SPIRITS LANDING · SOUTH-EAST GRAVESTONE

第二封：墓碑下的线索Second letter: clue beneath the gravestone

前往灵魂之地东南侧、地图上墓碑标记的位置。站在最大墓碑正上方使用下砸，进入隐藏房间并阅读下一封信。Go to the gravestone marker in south-east Spirits Landing. Stand above the largest grave and use Slam to enter the hidden room and read the next letter.

需要：下砸Requires: Slam
03
墓穴遗迹 · 千魂之墓首领房TOMB RUINS · TOMB OF 1000 SPIRITS ARENA

第三封：首领房上方的隐藏路Third letter: hidden path above the boss arena

回到千魂之墓的战斗房，沿左侧墙面向上攀爬，在房间顶部中央找到隐藏平台和信件。Return to the Tomb of 1000 Spirits arena. Climb the left wall and look for a hidden platform near the top centre of the room.

需要：攀墙Requires: Wall Climb
04
诺斯特布尔 · 第一座首领房右侧NORTHSTABLE · RIGHT OF THE FIRST BOSS ROOM

最终封信：回到最初的战场Final letter: return to the first arena

回到墓地爬行者首领房，前往获得二段跳的雕像右侧。按信件提示完成最后互动，结束寻宝线。Return to the Grave Crawler arena and go right of the statues where Double Jump was obtained. Follow the clue to complete the final interaction.

需要：二段跳Requires: Double Jump
