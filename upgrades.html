永久升级三选一思路

完成跑酷或战斗挑战会弹出三选一升级，全周目保留；不必追求"最优解"，按"刚在哪类挑战受挫"补对应短板最稳。

轻度剧透 最后校验：2026-09-01 阅读约 4 分钟
关键事实：游戏共有 60 个永久升级（成就 "Fully Equipped" 要求拿满）；每次挑战完成时随机出现 3 个，选其一，全周目保留。

常见升级类别

  • 生命（Health）：+1 心；最直观、降低受挫。
  • 剑（Sword）：提升单次伤害；打 Boss 时间更短。
  • 箭（Arrows）：射速、伤害、弹量升级；远程更稳。
  • 特殊能力：例如低血量时释放冲击波、击杀后回血、爆击时附带效果等。
  • 被动加成：拾取范围增大、跑动不掉箭、滞空更久等。

三种游玩风格的推荐

1. 新手向：稳扎稳打

  • 优先 生命：每两个升级里至少选 1 个心，目标是"前期 5–6 颗心、Boss 战前 7+ 颗"。
  • 次选 剑：和生命二选一时，缺血选血、不缺血选剑。
  • 避开"花活"型升级：等熟练了再考虑。

2. Boss 战向：求快

  • 优先 剑：把 Boss 战时长从 3 分钟压到 1 分半。
  • 次选 特殊能力：如"低血量冲击波"—— Boss 战关键时刻反杀用。
  • 只在打 Boss 前攒满 5–6 颗心，再开始挑战。

3. 全收集向：均衡

  • 心、剑、箭按 3 : 2 : 1 的比例分配，确保三项都不落后。
  • 拿到新能力后立刻回头扫荡隐藏房间，把附近未拿的升级一次性补完。
  • 中后期再开始考虑特殊能力，因为前 30 个升级决定基础盘。

进度参考

Steam 成就里有三个相关里程碑，可以当作"自己进度"的参考：

  • A Little Stronger：收集 1 个升级。
  • Pretty Tough!：收集 10 个升级。
  • Halfway There：收集 30 个升级。
  • Fully Equipped：收集 60 个升级。

三选一时的"避坑"

  1. 不要每次都选花活型升级；前 10 个升级尽量把基础项（生命、剑、箭）补齐。
  2. 不要刻意追求"先拿哪个能力"——Islets 升级不影响能力，能力来自主线剧情/挑战，升级只改数值/特殊效果。
  3. 不要"存"升级不选——Islets 没有"延迟选"机制，挑战完成后会立刻弹出三选一。
Key fact: Islets has 60 permanent upgrades in total (achievement "Fully Equipped"). Each challenge gives you a random pick of 3; the choice persists across playthroughs.

Common upgrade types

  • Health — +1 heart; simplest survivability boost.
  • Sword — higher per-hit damage; faster boss kills.
  • Arrows — fire rate, damage, or capacity; smoother ranged combat.
  • Special effects — e.g. shockwave at low HP, life steal on kill, crit procs.
  • Passives — pickup radius, no arrow drop in air, longer hang-time.

Three play-style recommendations

1. Beginner — play it safe

  • Pick Health first: at least 1 of every 2 upgrades should be a heart; aim for 5–6 hearts early, 7+ before boss fights.
  • Then Sword: if you don't need hearts, pick sword.
  • Skip "fancy" effects until you feel comfortable.

2. Boss-rush — finish fast

  • Prioritise Sword to compress boss fights from 3 minutes to 1.5.
  • Then Special effects like "low-HP shockwave" for clutch moments.
  • Build 5–6 hearts before challenging a boss.

3. Completionist — balanced

  • Aim for a 3 : 2 : 1 ratio of Health / Sword / Bow.
  • After every new ability, backtrack and sweep nearby rooms for missed upgrades.
  • Don't invest in fancy effects until the first 30 are in.

Progress milestones

  • A Little Stronger — collect 1 upgrade.
  • Pretty Tough! — collect 10 upgrades.
  • Halfway There — collect 30 upgrades.
  • Fully Equipped — collect 60 upgrades.

Common pitfalls

  1. Don't pick fancy effects every time; the first 10 should mostly fill Health / Sword / Bow.
  2. Don't chase "the right ability first" — upgrades are not abilities. Abilities come from story / story challenges.
  3. Don't "save" the choice — Islets pops the 3-way pick immediately after each challenge.
